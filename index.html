The Evolution of Movie Genres

How has the composition of film production changed over time? Explore the evolution of different movie genres between 1980 and 2025.

Each coloured band represents a movie genre. The thickness of the band indicates the number of TMDB-listed productions associated with that genre in a given year.

Reading the trend: The overall stream expands considerably in later years, indicating an increase in recorded genre credits. This reflects trends within the TMDB dataset and may also be influenced by changes in database coverage over time.

Source: TMDB. Each movie contributes one count to every genre assigned to it. Genre counts are therefore not mutually exclusive. Click a genre in the legend to highlight it.

Connecting the World Through Film

International film production connects countries through shared creative and financial partnerships. Explore the strongest co-production relationships recorded in TMDB between 1980 and 2025.

Each connection represents films jointly credited to two production countries. Thicker lines indicate more shared productions. Use the minimum shared films slider to filter weaker relationships, or select a country to explore its international co-production network.
