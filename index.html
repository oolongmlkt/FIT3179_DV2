01 / GLOBAL LANDSCAPE

Films Around the World

Explore where movies are produced, how genres have evolved, and how countries collaborate.

What does the map reveal?

Northern Europe stands out for its high recorded film-production intensity, particularly Iceland (72.56), Estonia (21.86) and Denmark (17.48) average annual movie credits per million residents. In contrast, the United States records a lower population-adjusted rate (9.17), despite having substantially more total movie credits. This demonstrates how adjusting for population reveals a different geographical pattern from comparing production volumes alone.

Source: TMDB movie production-country credits and World Bank population (SP.POP.TOTL). Released films, 1980-2025. A multi-country production contributes one credit to each credited country. Annual rates are averaged across years with valid population; missing values are not zero.

The Evolution of Movie Genres

How has the composition of film production changed over time? Explore different movie genres between 1980 and 2025.

Each coloured band represents a movie genre. Its thickness indicates the number of TMDB-listed productions associated with that genre in a given year.

Reading the trend: The overall stream expands considerably in later years, indicating an increase in recorded genre credits. This reflects trends within TMDB and may also be influenced by changes in database coverage over time.

Source: TMDB. Each movie contributes one count to every genre assigned to it; genre counts are not mutually exclusive. Click a genre in the legend to highlight it.
